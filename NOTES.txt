PALETTE — GOLD AND WHITE
------------------------
One accent, four weights, with strict rules. Gold on white is the
easiest thing to make illegible, so:

  --gold         #CFA43A   bright. DARK GROUNDS ONLY (2.3:1 on white)
  --gold-mid     #A87F18   rules, borders, display-size marks on white
  --gold-deep    #7A5A0F   any SMALL text on white, and solid fills
  --gold-darkest #5C420A   hover beneath a --gold-deep fill

Grounds are warm creams (--wash-1 #FAF6EC, --wash-2 #F4ECDA) and the
one dark band per page is a warm near-black brown (#241A0C), not the
old navy. Type is warm black (--ink #181206) over warm grey
(--body #4B4437).

Rule of thumb: --gold-mid at display sizes, --gold-deep below ~20px.
Everything currently on the site passes WCAG AA for its size.

The logo mark SVG is inline in all three HTML files and now strokes
#A87F18. If the wordmark is redrawn during the identity work, that's
the value to match.


FONTS
-----
Five faces, every one a real cut. No synthetic weights anywhere.
68 KB total, which is less than the single Absans file it replaced.

  Space Grotesk 700   headings, <strong>
  Space Grotesk 600   nav, buttons, eyebrows, tags, spec labels
  Open Sans 400       all running copy
  Newsreader Italic   the quote band and inline emphasis
  Absans (logo cut)   the ARTHANISA wordmark, nothing else

Absans stays on the wordmark on purpose: that's brand, not site type.
It's subset to the characters a wordmark can use, so 57 KB became 4 KB.
If the logo is redrawn as part of the identity work, this is the one
place to change.

Why the swap: Absans ships only Regular 400 with no italic, so every
heading was either thin or a browser-faked bold. Space Grotesk is a
close cousin of Absans with a genuine Bold, so the headings now have
real weight rather than an outline trick.

Optional: no .woff2 could be produced here (no brotli). Running these
through any woff2 converter and adding the woff2 line first in each
@font-face src would save roughly 25%.
