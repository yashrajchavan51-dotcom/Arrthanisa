About Us . . .

A meaningful niche in every home we shape.

ARTHANISA, the word, has its roots in the Sanskrit language — an amalgamation of two words: Arth, which means meaningful, and Nisa, which translates to home. We believe in creating meaningful, niche spaces in people’s lives — that’s their homes.

ARTHANISA is the brainchild of Ar. Meher Choupane, co-founded by Ar. Shirin Shaikh, based in Thane, Mumbai — with a view to provide quality architectural services under one roof, such as planning, designing, detailing and liaison services. Our approach prioritises simplicity, warmth and honesty.

Vision . . .

Every brief starts with how a space will be lived in, not how it will photograph. We plan for light, air and movement first and detail around them, so a building still works the way it was meant to a decade after handover.

The buildings we return to are the ones that were built honestly — clear structure, good proportion, materials left to be themselves. That is the standard the studio measures its own work against, on a single apartment as much as on an institutional site.

Expertise . . .

Planning, design, detailing and approvals handled under one roof.

1

Planning

Site, programme and buildable area established before anything is drawn — so the design that follows already fits the plot, the budget and the bye-laws.

2

Design

Light, air and movement resolved first, then the plan detailed around them. Simplicity and warmth over anything that only reads well in a rendering.

3

Detailing

Drawings a contractor can actually build from — junctions, finishes and services set out to the millimetre, checked on site through to handover.

4

Liaison & Approvals
